# Song from the comments

The radar is available only after the user enters, the cloud finishes forming, and the prepared song's exact source IDs and comment text match the loaded real collection. It is absent from the illustrative sample and incompatible imports. No transcript service or credential is shipped to the browser.

`SongExperience` owns a single HTML audio element, the gathering sequence, accessible transport, and source-preserving DOM. The existing sky keeps its fixed anchors. Visible source quotes animate from the actual projected comment locations into their places in song order. Reduced motion skips the flight. The recording plays after gathering; browsers that reject delayed playback expose an explicit Play recovery. Closing cancels pending arrival and playback, restores keyboard access to the sky, and preserves earlier score consent. Hiding the page pauses the song without resuming it automatically.

Highlights are derived from `audio.currentTime` on animation frames, with seek/time-update reconciliation. The provider's word start/end times are never rebuilt by dividing a phrase evenly. Added words reserve their positions while hidden, then appear at their start times, avoiding layout jumps. Exact source-character spans highlight within the unchanged full comment below each lyric. Other words in the comment remain intact. Ambiguous or absent source attribution is never presented as a confirmed comment match.

Display wording follows the user's supplied lyrics, including Romanized Hindi. Their spelling changes neither the raw MAI transcript nor the original comments. A Romanized display word can highlight the corresponding source word across scripts while preserving its provenance. When MAI split an acronym incorrectly or timed a whole spelled name as one word, its constituent interval is retained as a timed group; separate letter timings are not invented.

The recording is 135.66 seconds. Azure MAI-Transcribe-2 returned 218 word timings, with millisecond numeric resolution. These are model alignment estimates, not a claim of independently verified millisecond accuracy. The original MP3 was sent unchanged, preserving its silences and playback timeline. MAI's phrase offsets were zero, so lyric segment boundaries use their constituent word timestamps. The author's supplied lyrics resolve MAI's Hindi/Hinglish spelling and acronym errors. Recording repetitions and overlapping closing vocals remain explicitly documented in the alignment. The raw response, author-supplied lyrics, short-crop checks, and detailed matching notes remain under ignored `song-work/`.

Assets: `public/data/song.json`, `public/media/khushti.mp3`. Reproducible local preparation: `scripts/prepare-song.mjs`. The standalone builder embeds both assets; the source-only cinematic build contains neither. Updating unrelated comments does not remove a match, while a missing or changed source comment hides the feature rather than attaching words to the wrong person.
