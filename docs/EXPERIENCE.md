# From cloud to comment

The first sky consists of the reviewed comments, each with a stable place and complete original wording. Stars gather first, then resolve into the comment cloud. Click or tap once to approach a comment and a second time to read it. A guided wander or Find a moment also brings you to the words before opening them.

The selected words expand from their place in the cloud, accompanied by outward-moving starlight. They settle into a full-screen reading space. A comment's existing mood labels select its palette. Attribution, source link, and date stay visible. Known replies are expanded alongside the main comment; more comments from the same post or author offer a continuation.

Nearby words, reading history, and Another moment all open real archived records. Back to results restores the search. Escape or Back to this sky returns to the prior camera position. On small screens the reading space scrolls vertically with close and return controls kept accessible.

Reduced motion skips spatial expansion and shows the settled star field. Motion runs only during the bounded entrance. The source wording, whitespace, emoji, and reply relationships are preserved; missing archive context is never invented.
