## Stack
The user-supplied Cinematic Sky V front end uses WebGL with a Canvas2D fallback. The existing Vite application and local data collector remain the integration layer.

## Users and purpose
Khushi explores real comments people have left on her Instagram posts. From a distance, the comments form KHUSHI. Closer up, she can read them and follow conversations.

## Confirmed direction
The user chose a constellation of words and mood-based discovery, requested deeper development of that concept, and instructed us to start building as extraction continues. Preserve jokes, poetry, teasing, conversational remarks and specific appreciation. Exclude emoji-only reactions and filler; keep short compliments from other people to populate the complete name. Preserve original wording and attribution.

## Constraints
Work in this isolated directory. Read the adjacent reviewed archive without modifying it. Start as a local working preview; hosting and a final complete dataset are separate pending decisions. No fabricated comments in the real archive. A separately labelled sample sky is available only through explicit selection when no archive is loaded. Counts represent unique comments, not visual text fragments. Comments by Khushi are excluded from the cloud, reading list, related comments, and conversation context.

## Evidence
../instagram-comments-khushi-2026-09-16/khushi-comments-filtered.json provides the reviewed core, supplemented from adjacent snapshots by the user-requested relaxed filter (346 received comments). Coverage has known Instagram visibility gaps and must be reported accurately. Automatic filtering and mood tags are provisional editorial aids.

## Accessibility
Keyboard-accessible reading list, motion pause, reduced-motion preference, mobile tap/pinch support, and readable HTML for focused comments.
