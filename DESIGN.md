# Living Sky visual direction

Mode: Experience. Points of starlight drift into place over 5.8 seconds and resolve into the real comments that form KHUSHI. Comment text does not fly across the opening; it appears at its permanent position as the stars settle. Camera movement reveals the words without changing their positions. Georgia/Times serif and the existing system sans stack carry the original Living Sky identity.

A first click preserves the cinematic zoom; only the second click opens the reader. Opening a comment is the focal interaction: its actual words travel from their projected location into readable HTML while a finite burst of stars expands around them. It settles within roughly one second. The reading space occupies the viewport, retaining the sky instead of introducing a paper card. Original words lead; the saved thread and nearby real comments sit alongside on desktop and below on mobile.

The reader uses warm starlight by default, rose for love, amber for laughter, and blue for poetry, chosen from provisional mood metadata. Secondary type remains readable. The star field becomes static after the entrance; reduced motion immediately displays the settled reader. Sound requires explicit opt-in.

The core sky, search, minimap, guided journeys, local saved marks, camera retrace, and comfort controls remain available. Closing the reader returns to the previous place. No generated praise or sample comments ship in the interface.
