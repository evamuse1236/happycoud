# Cinematic Sky visual direction

Mode: Experience. Preserve the user-supplied Cinematic Sky V: one continuous sky, warm starlight on deep blue-black, Georgia/Times serif original words, and quiet system-sans controls. Fixed comment anchors emerge within condensing dust; neither arrival nor reading scatters individual words. Reduced motion settles immediately.

A first click or tap approaches the actual comment. A second selection of those words opens the complete original in selectable HTML through a whole-paragraph dissolve. Enter follows the same two steps. The saved conversation and related archive comments share the sky-backed reading space, alongside on desktop and below on mobile. Related comments never masquerade as thread replies. Closing restores the saved camera position; manual movement cancels selection.

A single edge light reveals a small constellation containing Find a voice, Feeling loved, Make me laugh, A little poetry, Show everything, Kept close, and Make this yours. Mood selection changes brightness without rearranging the sky. A miniature home map appears after leaving the wide view.

The opt-in procedural piano and sustained harmonic score continues through navigation without interface cues. While a feeling is selected, a recurring instrumental part joins its current harmony: cello-like for love, marimba-like for laughter, flute-like for poetry. Switching feelings crossfades that part without resetting the score; Show everything removes it. Reading softens it but does not stop its phrases. Mute and hidden-page suspension apply to all layers. Piano-only mode intentionally omits extra instruments.

Visual reference: the user's Downloads/happycoud-cinematic-v5-source.zip. Preserve its spacious composition, starlight palette, restrained controls, full original words, continuous scene, and attention-based sound. Its glow is intentional lighting, not a generic decoration to remove.

When a feeling is active, a quiet Show everything control remains beside the edge light. It clears the feeling and returns to the whole sky. Fine-pointer devices use a small native starlight cursor, a brighter clickable star, and a directional drag star; selectable text retains its normal text cursor.
